# Kalamazoo Local Government

A directory for live streams, recordings, agendas, and minutes across Kalamazoo County local government — all in one place.

Every municipality in Kalamazoo County uses a different platform for their meeting videos and documents. This site stops the scavenger hunt.

---

|---|---|---|

---

## Streaming Platforms

- **Public Media Network** — Kalamazoo City Commission, Kalamazoo Township, Comstock Township, Oshtemo Township
- **YouTube** — Kalamazoo County Commission
- **Cablecast** — Portage City Council
- **In person only** — Parchment City Commission
