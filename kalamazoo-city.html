Policy 10-1 · Updated November 12, 2024
Regular Business Meetings
General comments: 3 min. During the Public Comments section: 4 min. Time may be extended by the chair or by vote.
Committee of the Whole / Special Meetings
Limited to matters on that meeting’s agenda. Individual speakers: 2 min.
Identification Required
State your name and whether you reside in the City of Kalamazoo.
What You Can Speak To
All action items on the agenda, except Consent Agenda items, votes setting a public hearing, or secondary motions.
Written Proposals
File with the City Clerk. Included in a Clerk’s agenda report within 21 days of receipt.
